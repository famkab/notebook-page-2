  1. 1KS3 · EVIDENCEDYear 9 options forkSubjects are chosen before most young people have met the jobs they lead to. Little feels decided, but the runway has started.
  2. 2KS4 · EVIDENCEDMock examsPredicted grades start deciding what a young person can apply for.
  3. 3KS4 · EVIDENCEDPost-16 deadlinesApplications land while Progress 8 pulls schools toward academic routes. The highest-pressure point in the map.
  4. 4KS4 · EVIDENCEDThe summer gapResults, then a gap with no one holding the thread. The September Guarantee sits here as a local authority duty.
  5. 5KS5 · EVIDENCEDMaths and English re-sitsLevel 2 maths and English become a condition of funding for continued study.
  6. 6KS5 · EVIDENCEDUCAS?UCASThe Universities and Colleges Admissions Service, the single system through which people apply to UK universities. and apprenticeship?APPRENTICESHIPA paid job with training built in, leading to a recognised qualification.   7. 7KS5 · INFERRED, NEEDS CONFIRMINGRisk of NEET?NEETNot in education, employment or training. The UK term for a young person who has left school and has none of the three.Where a young person falls out with nothing to fall back on. Drawn as inferred: the data to confirm it sits in Compass+ and local authority returns.
