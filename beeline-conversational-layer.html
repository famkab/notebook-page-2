NOTE 02 · ARTEFACT 6

Conversational layer

The layer sits on Build Your Future, a planning board where a young person lays out the steps from the subjects they take now to the job they aim at. It reads the board and speaks about what is on it.

THE NUDGE RULE

01 · A TAGShort and pinned to the stage it is about, so the young person sees where it applies.
02 · ONE PLAIN LINEWhat the layer noticed on the board, in words a fourteen-year-old would use.
03 · ONE ACTIONA single next step they can take from the board.
⟡ Suggested cardsCards the layer proposes are marked, so the young person can tell their own plan from a suggestion and choose whether to keep it.
Shared viewAn adult, such as a careers adviser or a parent, can open the same board and talk it through with them.
