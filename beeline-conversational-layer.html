NOTE 02 · ARTEFACT 6

AI Conversational layer

The layer sits on Build Your Future, a planning board where a young person lays out the steps from the subjects they take now to the job they aim at. It reads the board and speaks about what is on it. The diamond marks everything it adds.

HOW I USE AI IN A PRODUCTIt has to be useful, and tied to a problem it solves. Ideally it is a layer on top of a solution, not the whole solution itself.

DESIGN NOTES · FROM THE SKETCH

  1. AOne selector for readiness and intent. It replaces ‘I want to…’.
  2. BGateways come from the Discovery Quiz. Numbers on cards show overlaps.
  3. COther features add cards, so the board holds the whole journey.
  4. DExperiences saved from Our Pass, like Palace Theatre backstage.
  5. EJobs saved from the Employer Hub.
  6. FNudges sit on the card they refer to: a tag, one line, one action.
  7. GWhy this matters: each stage explains itself, open or closed.
  8. HThe selector sets the stage focus and highlights matching cards.
  9. IPlan so far, shared with an adult when they choose.
