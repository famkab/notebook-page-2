NOTE 01 · LEARNING METERFROM P. 6–11

Ten words that tell a learner what to fix

Feedback is read mid-game, so it had to be short, quick and honest. Ten words or fewer, in the first person, naming one fix, and strict on the score. False praise was treated as a risk and measured.

NOTE 02 · BEELINEFROM P. 16–21

Nudges on a young person’s plan

The conversational layer reads the Planner Board and speaks about what is on it. Each nudge is a tag, one plain line and one action. Suggested cards are marked ⟡, so a young person can tell their own plan from a suggestion.
