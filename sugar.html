NOTE 04 · A TECHNOLOGY WITH NO MARKET YET

The Artificial Leaf

An academic–industry innovation programme run on SUGAR’s Triple Diamond?TRIPLE DIAMONDSUGAR’s design process: three rounds of opening out and narrowing down, used to find where a technology could go, test it, and reframe the problem., with a six-person team of students from the University of Bologna and the Université Côte d’Azur. I was hired by the Innovation Centre for Entrepreneurship. The Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia was the client.

THE BRIEF

What the institute asked for

WHAT THEY HADAn artificial photosynthesis system turning CO₂ into carbon monoxide, formic acid, methanol, ethanol and ethylene using solar energy. Built at lab scale, with no market.
THE CHALLENGE, AS WRITTENHow might we develop an innovative service based on the “Artificial photosynthesis technology” and create a business opportunity out of it?
THE METHOD · THREE WORLDS

Three diamonds, diverge and converge each time

  1. ◇ DIAMOND 1 · AUTUMNWhere could it go?Desk research across nine fields, case studies of comparable technologies, expert interviews, personas.
  2. ◇ DIAMOND 2 · WINTERDoes it work there?Plastics taken forward. Critical experience prototypes, then dark horse and funky prototypes, then one merged functional system.
  3. ◆ DIAMOND 3 · SPRINGWhat is the real problem?The challenge turned around. Design vision, solution, brand and interface, built and tested against a real case.

NINE FIELDS, NARROWED TWICE

ChimneysCement productionFashionBuilding sectorTransportation★ Plastic upcyclingAgricultureDigital pollutionWaste sector

Pass one used three guiding features: desirability, technical feasibility, economic viability. Pass two used eight selection criteria, voted on directly on a mind map of every application we had found. Plastics upcycling was taken forward.

PLOT TWIST · THE U-TURN

The deadlock underneath

THE SCIENTISTSNeed companies to invest so the technology can be developed further.
⇄
THE COMPANIESNeed the technology developed further before they are comfortable investing.
THE CHALLENGE, REWRITTENHow might we create a reliable system around the artificial leaf technology during its development phase in order to onboard companies?

WHAT WE DELIVERED · CO/LAB

Software that simulates installing the leaf in a specific factory, so the institute and a company can work through the technical, physical and financial logistics together, on editable templates, in the same room.

  1. 01 · SURVEY ✓Emissions, emissions taxes, space and the output wanted.
  2. 02 · TECHNICAL SIMULATION ✓Location and output volume produce a detailed simulation.
  3. 03 · FINANCIAL ESTIMATION ✓A benefits evaluation and a costs-and-benefits analysis.
  4. 04 · 3D MODEL · LOCKEDSpecified, and left as the next build.
