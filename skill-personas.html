PROJECT 01 · BEELINE · 2026FROM P. 3–8

Dylan, the Overwhelmed Explorer

One of five research-validated personas, observed across young people with inconsistent engagement, including some not in education, employment or training, and young people with limited support networks. Four of the five personas feel overwhelmed when they explore and filter options, so Dylan stands in for the biggest group. He is also the furthest from Clare, the one persona who feels in control.

“Looking for careers information online is overwhelming, I don’t know where to start and when I do find something I don’t think I have the qualities to be able to do it.”
OPPORTUNITY STATEMENTHelp young people navigate opportunities without becoming overwhelmed, by simplifying information, reducing friction and providing clear next steps.

KEY STAGE 2 · YEAR 6
At 10 I am … just starting to wonder what jobs are out there. In Year 6 in Oldham. He loves building in Minecraft. Nobody he knows works with computers, so jobs feel like something other people do. We want him to see that what he enjoys links to real jobs.

PROJECT 04 · THE ARTIFICIAL LEAF · 2021–2022FROM P. 19–21

A persona for every industry we interviewed

After interviews with companies, experts and researchers, the team drew up a persona for each field. Each one summarised the needs of a company or a larger entity, and each led to a How Might We question and a first prototype of where the leaf could fit. Plastics was taken forward.

CEMENT PRODUCTION
SebastiánEnvironmental engineer and carbon capture lead in a cement production factory
MAIN NEEDSTurn carbon dioxide into an opportunity and avoid fees · keep production running 24 hours
HOW MIGHT WEHow might we help Sebastián use the artificial leaf to intervene with carbon dioxide storage during the production of cement, in order to add value to it?
PLASTICSTAKEN FORWARD
ThomasCEO of a plastic manufacturing company, with a good overview of how recyclable a broad range of materials are
MAIN NEEDSA better waste separation system · less new plastic, to avoid plastic taxes
HOW MIGHT WEHow might we use the artificial leaf to decrease the amount of new plastic that is needed, which results in less plastic tax to pay?
MARITIME SHIPPING
IngridOwner of a marine shipping company, facing strict environmental rules in transport
MAIN NEEDSFuel that complies with new policies on carbon dioxide emissions
HOW MIGHT WEHow might we create a more sustainably produced fuel for the marine shipping industry, to comply with new policies on carbon emissions?
WASTE TO ENERGY
LucaManager of a waste-to-energy plant who wants to invest in innovation for the company’s future
MAIN NEEDSReduce the carbon dioxide emitted when burning waste · a circular, efficient system
HOW MIGHT WEHow might we add value to the carbon dioxide emitted during the burning phase, to give the company a new market opportunity?
FOOD PRODUCTION
EricaHealth and safety, environment and energy department of a food company
MAIN NEEDSRecognition for the company’s sustainable activities · access to investment
HOW MIGHT WEHow might we help food companies gain recognition for their sustainable activities and speed up their energy transition?
