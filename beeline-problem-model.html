THE TEN CHALLENGES · HOW MIGHT WE…

  1. C1 redirect people through our services without them losing confidence or feeling dismissed?
  2. C2 separate exploring jobs from building a pathway, so the gateway pages stop being where the journey collapses?
  3. C3 support a journey that’s messy and non-linear, where exploring and deciding happen at the same time?
  4. C4 reduce what a young person has to hold in their head at any one point?
  5. C5 say what we’re obliged to say in far fewer words, without losing the meaning?
  6. C6 make it obvious that what a young person is reading is true and current?
  7. C7 make the Unplugged offline toolkits and the website hand off to each other properly?
  8. C8 support the adults around a young person, not only the young person?
  9. C9 help a young person see what they can now do that they couldn’t before?
  10. C10 make encounters with employers feel real and useful?
