European Digital Education Hub

Two squads with the European Commission’s Digital Education Hub, across a year. One asked how data can inform the design of learning spaces. The other asked how public–private partnerships shape AI adoption in education across Europe.

Learning and space analytics Dublin opened with a study visit to Trinity College Dublin, then empathy mapping and scenario design. I sat in the sub-group on data-informed design and wrote the section on learning and space analytics: what a space can reasonably tell you about learning, and where the line sits between designing with data and surveilling the people in the room.

SQUAD 2 · AI ADOPTION IN EUROPE · BRUSSELS
Public–private partnerships and AI adoption The arrangements are opaque, they vary by country, and the people they affect most have the least visibility of them. The group built a set of game-like cards that walk someone through a partnership and make the policy space legible to the people who have to live with it.

Where it went next
