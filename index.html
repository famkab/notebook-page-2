PROJECT 01GREATER MANCHESTER COMBINED AUTHORITY

Beeline

The systems under a public service

Beeline is the young person’s version of the MBacc, Greater Manchester’s framework of technical and academic routes into work, which became national policy in July 2026. I design its planner board and the AI layer over it, with suggested cards and nudges, and underneath them the service landscape, the problem model and the metric framework.
