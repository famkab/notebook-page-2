PROJECT 01GREATER MANCHESTER COMBINED AUTHORITY

Beeline

A careers service for young people in Greater Manchester

Beeline is how we speak to young people about the MBacc, Greater Manchester’s framework of technical and academic routes into work, which became national policy in July 2026. I designed the Discovery Quiz and I am designing the Planner Board and its AI layer of suggested cards and nudges. The service landscape, problem model and metrics framework sit underneath and contribute to product strategy, prioritisation and feature definition.

PROJECT 02KIDS&US

Learning Meter

AI feedback on learners’ spoken and written English

An AI feature in Animal Planet by Kids&Us that scores learners’ spoken and written English and gives them short feedback they can act on. I designed the rubric, the prompts and the evaluation that ran before launch, which cut false passes from 58% to 27%.

PROJECT 03JUMP MATH

JUMP Math Lab

A maths lab that adapts to each student

In JUMP Math Lab, a student’s results decide which path they take next. I designed the forked pathway and the teacher dashboard, which shows each student’s progress and what to do next lesson.

The Artificial Leaf

Finding a market for a lab technology
