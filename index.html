Entry 02 · p. 8 · Greater Manchester Combined Authority

Beeline

The systems under a public service

Beeline is the young person’s version of the MBacc, Greater Manchester’s framework of technical and academic routes into work, which became national policy in July 2026. I design its planner board and the AI layer over it, with suggested cards and nudges, and underneath them the service landscape, the problem model and the metric framework that decides what the layer may claim.

  • SectorPublic
  • ScaleRegional · 10 local authorities
  • AudienceYoung people up to 25
  • ProductPublic service, online and offline
